# Group 2 tasks

h = hours for the whole group. B = Bachelor, M = Master-led, B+M = shared.
Every student: 10 h onboarding + 75 h core tasks + 35 h meetings, report, talk = 120 h.
Start in week 1: T0, T1, T6, T8.
Group 1's tasks are in the `ai-copilot-sim` repository.

| # | task | goal | input → output | done when | h | lvl | depends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T0 | **Onboarding** | Get the environment running and understand the claim-check engine. | In: code export. Out: everyone runs the demo. | Every student runs the demo on their laptop and passes a 5-question quiz on the data rule. | 60 (6×10) | B+M | none · week 1 |
| T1 | **Scenario catalogue** | Define what a planted mistake is and which answer is correct. | In: synthetic generator. Out: scenarios file, ground-truth schema, one driving card per scenario. | At least 24 scenarios (brake early, late throttle, coasting, plus no-mistake cases). A fixed seed regenerates identical files. Every scenario has a one-line driving card for the simulator. | 30 | B+M | T0 · week 1 |
| T2 | **Benchmark harness** | Run the checker over all scenarios and seeds, automatically. | In: scenarios. Out: confusion matrix, truth × answer. | One command reproduces the table bit for bit. CI runs a small subset. | 50 | B | T1 · starter code |
| T3 | **Metrics** | Define how “right” is measured. | In: harness output. Out: metrics module: accuracy, wrong-answer rate, “Can't tell” rate, empirical vs nominal 90 % coverage. | Unit tests against hand-computed cases. The report tables are generated by this module. | 30 | B+M | T2 (start on mock output) |
| T4 | **Degradation sweeps** | Break the data in controlled ways and see where the checker fails. | In: clean files. Out: 4 curves: right-answer rate vs noise, lap count, sampling rate, GPS error. | Each factor at ≥ 6 levels × ≥ 30 seeds. Curves with intervals are committed. Squad A: noise, lap count. Squad B: rate, GPS. | 60 | B | T2 |
| T5 | **Tolerance calibration** | Tune tolerances and interval to cut wrong answers at an acceptable “Can't tell” rate. | In: benchmark results. Out: thresholds v2 + short write-up. | Tuning set ≠ test set. v2 beats v1 on the held-out set by a criterion written down before the run. | 60 | M | T3, T4 |
| T6 | **Generator realism ladder** | Add tyre wear, traffic and driver variability as switchable levels L0–L3. | In: generator. Out: switchable effects. | Each effect is off by default, tested, and documented with its effect on the checker. | 70 | B+M | T0 · week 1 |
| T7 | **Fidelity metric** | Measure how far a synthetic session is from a simulated one. | In: two session sets. Out: one distance, built from per-lap metric distributions. | Stable across seeds. Shows whether L3 is closer to the simulator than L0, or why not. | 40 | M | T6, Group 1's S6 |
| T8 | **Interface contract and validator** | Write the lap-format contract with Group 1 and a validator their recorder must pass. | In: draft LAP-FORMAT.md, Group 1's channel list. Out: agreed LAP-FORMAT v0 + validator. | Contract agreed with Group 1 by 13 Nov. Validator rejects missing channels, non-monotonic time, and files without source and receive time; tested on good and broken files. Group 1's output passes it. | 40 | B+M | T0 · starter code |
| T9 | **Timing-fault sweeps** | See how timing faults change the checker's answers. | In: clean files, Group 1's latency-model ranges. Out: curves: jitter, dropped samples, clock offset between channels, duplicate or out-of-order rows. | 4 faults × ≥ 6 levels × ≥ 30 seeds, and a table of which metric breaks first. Levels match Group 1's model once available. | 30 | B | T4, Group 1's S7 |
| T10 | **Transfer experiment** | Compare the benchmark on synthetic and simulator sessions: how much of the guarantee holds? | In: results of T5–T9, Group 1's labelled sessions. Out: one figure and a conclusion. | Result with intervals, including the case where it does not transfer. | 40 | M | T5, T7, Group 1's S6 |
| T11 | **Meetings, report, final talk** | Weekly meetings, a short report, the final presentation. | In: all results. Out: report ≤ 12 pages + talk. | Report reviewed by Luke. Talk rehearsed once. | 210 (6×35) | B+M | all |
